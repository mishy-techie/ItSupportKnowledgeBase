## 📊 Troubleshooting Methodology

A major focus of this knowledge base is developing a structured approach to troubleshooting.

My general troubleshooting process is:

```text
1. Identify the problem
        ↓
2. Gather information
        ↓
3. Reproduce the issue
        ↓
4. Identify possible causes
        ↓
5. Test the most likely cause
        ↓
6. Apply the appropriate solution
        ↓
7. Verify the resolution
        ↓
8. Document the outcome
```

The goal is to avoid simply applying fixes and instead understand **why an issue occurred and how to prevent it from happening again**.
